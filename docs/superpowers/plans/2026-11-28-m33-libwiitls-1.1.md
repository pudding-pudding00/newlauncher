## Global Constraints
- Update `third_party/wii_tls` **only** via `python tools/vendor_wii_tls.py <checkout> --rev v1.1.0`.
- Every `wtls_*` call stays strictly on the `NetService` thread; never fall back from HTTPS to HTTP; never disable certificate verification.
- Production `build/wii/newlauncher.dol` MUST have `WTLS_DOLPHIN_DEV_ENTROPY=OFF` and `WTLS_KEYLOG=OFF` (enforced by CMake defaults, `make_release.py`, and negative Dolphin smoke).
- 0 warnings on all three Wii builds (`build_wii.py`, `--no-tls`, `--dolphin-dev-tls`).
